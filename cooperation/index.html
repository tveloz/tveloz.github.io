Research Participation Consent

This research studies semantic password systems. Your participation is voluntary and data will be anonymized for research purposes.

We collect: interaction patterns, response times, success rates, and anonymized story elements. No personal information or actual stories are stored. Data will be used for academic research only.

How It Works

Setup Phase: Share a detailed personal story, memory, or fictional character
Recognition Phase: Provide a brief "Remember when..." style summary
Verification Phase: Answer specific questions about story elements
Authentication: Access granted based on story recognition + precise details
