Philosophy of Interdisciplinary Work

Interdisciplinarity is not merely the combination of different disciplines—it is the emergence of new knowledge that transcends the boundaries of individual fields. My approach to interdisciplinary work is rooted in the belief that the most pressing challenges of our time require integrated perspectives that no single discipline can provide alone.

Through the DICTA Foundation and my academic work, I have developed methodologies for creating truly transdisciplinary spaces where different ways of knowing—scientific, artistic, philosophical, and experiential—can dialogue and co-create new understanding.

This work requires not just intellectual flexibility, but a fundamental commitment to epistemic humility: recognizing that each discipline offers partial truths, and that integration requires careful attention to both conceptual translation and the preservation of domain-specific insights.

Philosophical Interests:

DICTA Foundation: Interdisciplinarity in Action

The Foundation for Interdisciplinary Development of Science, Technology and Arts (DICTA) serves as a living laboratory for interdisciplinary practice. Through DICTA, I have developed concrete methodologies for bringing together diverse communities of practice around shared challenges.

Our work demonstrates that true interdisciplinarity requires not just intellectual integration, but also attention to social dynamics, power relationships, and the cultural contexts in which knowledge is created and applied.
