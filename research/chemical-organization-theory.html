Chemical Organization Theory

Developing mathematical frameworks for analyzing self-organizing systems using reaction networks

What is Chemical Organization Theory?

Chemical Organization Theory (COT) provides a mathematical framework for understanding self-organizing systems through the lens of reaction networks. It emerged from the need to formalize how complex systems maintain their structure and identity over time despite constant flux in their components.

At its core, COT identifies "organizations" - subsets of species that are both closed (all reactions stay within the set) and self-maintaining (the set can be sustained given sufficient resource flow). These mathematical structures help us understand everything from metabolic networks to social systems.

Key Concepts

  • Closure: Reactions within the organization
  • Self-maintenance: Sustainable production
  • Hierarchies: Organizations within organizations
  • Emergence: System-level properties
