Foundations of Inter/Transdisciplinarity

Understanding the theoretical and practical foundations of interdisciplinary collaboration

Bridging Disciplines for Complex Challenges

As founder and director of the Foundation for Interdisciplinary Development of Science, Technology and Arts (DICTA), Dr. Veloz has pioneered research into the theoretical foundations of interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary collaboration. This work addresses how knowledge integration occurs across disciplinary boundaries.

The research explores the philosophical, methodological, and practical aspects of team science, examining how diverse disciplinary perspectives can be effectively integrated to address complex global challenges. Through DICTA, this work has generated social impact projects that bridge academic research with real-world applications.

Key Research Dimensions

  • Epistemology: How knowledge is integrated across fields
  • Team Science: Collaborative research methodologies
  • Global South: Promoting southern hemisphere research
  • Arts Integration: Science, technology, and arts synthesis
