Quantum Cognition

Applying quantum mathematical structures to model cognitive and linguistic phenomena

What is Quantum Cognition?

Quantum Cognition applies the mathematical formalism of quantum theory to cognitive science, without claiming that the brain operates quantum mechanically. This approach uses quantum structures like Hilbert spaces, superposition, and entanglement as powerful tools to model human reasoning, decision-making, and concept formation.

Dr. Veloz has developed methods to represent collections of concepts in Hilbert spaces and demonstrated that various cognitive and linguistic phenomena exhibit quantum-like structures. This includes modeling the conjunction fallacy, order effects in judgment, and contextuality in human reasoning.

Key Quantum Concepts

  • Superposition: Mental states as probability combinations
  • Entanglement: Non-separable concept relationships
  • Contextuality: Context-dependent reasoning
  • Interference: Non-classical probability effects
