# Semantic Password Research Study

## Participant Information

This research studies a new type of password system based on personal stories rather than traditional character sequences.

### What you'll do:
1. **Setup** (5-10 minutes): Share a personal story or memory
2. **Verification** (2-3 minutes): Verify your identity using story elements
3. **Questions** (if needed): Answer specific details about your story

### Data Collection:
- Response times and interaction patterns
- Success rates and confidence scores  
- Anonymized story elements (no personal content)
- User experience feedback

### Privacy:
- All data is anonymized
- No personal stories or identifying information stored
- You can download your data at any time
- Participation is voluntary and you can stop anytime
